Tired, but wired?

Upeksha Perera has taught Kundalini yoga since 2017. Her classes pair breathwork and gentle movement with live gong, singing bowls and voice, so your nervous system can finally let go.

Every session is safe and beginner friendly. Come as you are. A weekly reset, not another thing to get right.
